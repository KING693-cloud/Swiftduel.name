Frequently Asked

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How do I deposit?
Go to the Deposit page, copy the PalmPay account details, generate a narration code, send your amount (min ₦500), and submit the form with your sender name. Admin approves within 24 hours.
What is the narration code?
A unique 10‑character code generated for each deposit session. You must include it in your bank transfer description.
How long is the contract?
All VIP plans run for 365 days (1 year). When you upgrade to a higher VIP tier, the counter resets.
Can I withdraw anytime?
Yes, withdrawals are processed Monday–Saturday, 10 AM – 6 PM (WAT). Minimum withdrawal is ₦1,000 for referral, and task withdrawals follow admin‑set schedule.
How does the referral bonus work?
When someone registers with your referral link and buys a VIP plan, you earn 45% of their plan price as a one‑time commission.
Is my money safe?
Absolutely. All funds are held in licensed accounts with PalmPay. We use bank‑grade encryption and daily reconciliation.
How do I upgrade my VIP plan?
From your dashboard, select the VIP plan you want to upgrade to. Your contract resets to 365 days from the upgrade date.
What happens after 365 days?
Your contract completes and you can choose to renew, upgrade, or withdraw your earnings. You keep all earnings forever.
